PHOTOS FOR THE FOOD & STAYS PAGE
================================

Put one photo per food place in this folder, named after the place id, ending in .jpg.
The site picks them up automatically. No code change is needed.

  cafe-goodluck.jpg
  vaishali.jpg
  bedekar.jpg
  sujata-mastani.jpg
  kayani-bakery.jpg
  chitale-bandhu.jpg

Tips
- Landscape photos, about 1200 x 800. Keep each file under about 300 KB.
  The free tool https://squoosh.app shrinks photos without visible loss.
- Use photos you took or have the right to use. Do not copy images from
  Google results or other websites.
- Until a photo is added, a clean coloured placeholder is shown instead.
- To add a new food place, add it to src/data/places.js. Its photo name is its id,
  for example a place with id 'katakirr-misal' uses katakirr-misal.jpg.
